## **What Problems Businesses Face with Voice AI**

Many businesses struggle when integrating voice AI, not because the idea is weak but because real-world challenges often get in the way.

-   **Language diversity:** Supporting multiple regional and global languages can be difficult. Many AI systems still find it hard to understand accents or local dialects.
-   **Latency and response quality:** Delayed or robotic replies make conversations feel unnatural. Real interactions need speed, accuracy, and clear tone.
-   **Scalability:** Managing large call volumes without losing quality requires strong infrastructure and consistent performance.
-   **Data privacy and control:** Voice AI platforms must protect sensitive information such as financial or medical data while maintaining compliance standards.

These challenges highlight the need for a platform that balances technology, reliability, and ease of use.

## ****Bolna vs Retell AI: Key Comparisons****

Both Bolna and Retell are designed to make voice automation easier for businesses, but the way they perform and the features they offer are quite different. Here are some of the key areas where Bolna and Retell differ.

### **1\. Pricing and Scalability**

Bolna offers a cost-effective pricing model at around $0.03 per minute, making it ideal for companies that handle a large number of calls each day. Retell, on the other hand, costs about $0.10 per minute, which can make scaling more expensive over time.

Bolna also supports advanced batch calling and provides ready-made agents for multiple industries, helping teams grow without technical challenges. Retell’s scalability is limited by fewer features and integration options, which can slow down expansion for larger businesses.

### **2\. Customization and Functionality**

Customization is one of the strongest differences between Bolna and Retell. Bolna offers a simple bot builder powered by OpenAI, allowing users to adjust prompts and workflows without any coding experience. This makes it easy to create voice agents that reflect a company’s tone and communication style.

Bolna also provides a wide range of pre-built templates for both inbound and outbound use cases, including recruitment, healthcare, real estate, and food services. Teams can deploy these agents quickly and make small edits to fit their needs.

Retell, in comparison, offers basic manual setup options and lacks built-in templates or advanced customization tools. For companies that want more control and flexibility, Bolna delivers a smoother and more complete experience.

### **3\. Performance and Reporting**

Performance plays a key role in how effective a voice AI system can be. Bolna provides faster and smoother conversations with latency between 500 and 800 milliseconds, allowing calls to feel natural and uninterrupted. Retell’s latency ranges between 1100 and 1200 milliseconds, which can lead to slower or less responsive interactions.

Bolna also includes detailed analytics and reporting features directly on the platform. Users can view call summaries, transcripts, and recordings in one place, helping teams track performance and make improvements easily. Retell offers only limited reporting, such as basic call duration and latency data, giving users less insight into overall quality and performance.

### **4\. Support and Setup Experience**

Customer support and ease of setup can make a major difference in how quickly a business gets value from voice AI. Bolna provides 24-hour live chat, onboarding calls, and personalized demo sessions to guide users through setup and agent creation. Most agents can be deployed within minutes, even by non-technical users.

Retell offers limited support options, mainly through email and Discord, which can slow down troubleshooting. Its setup process takes longer and often requires manual configuration. Bolna’s intuitive interface and quick-start tools make it easier for any team to begin using voice automation with confidence and minimal effort.

### **5\. Security and Data Privacy**

Security plays a crucial role when businesses manage customer data through voice AI. Bolna ensures that all call data, transcripts, and recordings are handled securely and remain protected within the platform. It also follows strong data protection standards, giving companies more confidence when dealing with sensitive information.

Retell offers basic data management but does not provide advanced security options or detailed control over data storage. For organizations that work in regulated sectors or prioritize compliance, Bolna provides a safer and more transparent environment for managing voice data.
