## **How Bolna Plivo Integration Works**

A Plivo phone number is an online business number that works over the internet instead of a physical phone line. Many companies use Plivo numbers to make and receive calls easily, without needing extra hardware or complex setups.

If your business already uses Plivo, you can connect that number directly to your Bolna account. This lets your Bolna voice AI agent answer customer calls automatically, providing instant, friendly support at any hour.

![Bolna Plivo Integration](/images/posts/bolna-plivo-integration/veewfwee-1024x576.jpg)

If you don’t yet have a Plivo account, no problem, you can quickly sign up on the Plivo website and get a virtual phone number for your region. Once your number is active, follow these simple steps to connect your Plivo number with Bolna.

### **Step 1: Copy Your Inbound Link from Bolna**

1.  Log in to your [Bolna Dashboard](https://platform.bolna.ai/login).
2.  Open the AI agent you want to use for incoming calls.
3.  On the agent’s page, find the Inbound API URL. This is the link that lets Plivo send calls to your agent.
4.  Copy this link. You will need it in the next step.

![](/images/posts/bolna-plivo-integration/Screenshot-2025-10-14-at-8.49.45-AM-1024x681.png)

[Source](https://platform.bolna.ai/)

### **Step 2: Add the Link in Plivo**

1.  Log in to your Plivo account.
2.  Go to XML Applications and click Create New Application.
3.  Name it something simple, like Bolna Inbound Agent.
4.  In the boxes labeled Primary Answer URL and Hangup URL, paste the Inbound API URL you copied from Bolna.
5.  Click Save to confirm. This step allows Plivo to direct incoming calls from your number to your Bolna agent.

### **Step 3: Connect the Application to Your Phone Number**

1.  Go to Active Numbers in your Plivo dashboard.
2.  Choose the phone number you want to use.
3.  Under settings, select the application you just created.
4.  Click Update Number or Save.

### **Step 4: Test Your **Bolna Plivo Integration** Setup**

1.  Call your Plivo number from any phone.
2.  Your Bolna voice AI agent should answer, greet you, and respond as configured.

If your agent answers and responds, everything is set up correctly. Your Bolna account is now connected to your Plivo number, ready to handle Indian voice automation customer calls, in real time, and in the language your customers prefer.
