## **How Hiring Changed Over Time**

The way companies hire has gone through many changes over the years. In the past, the process was slow and fully manual. Businesses collected printed resumes, made phone calls one by one, and scheduled interviews through long chains of emails. Finding the right person often took weeks or even months.

As online job portals and digital forms became popular, hiring started to move faster. Recruiters could reach more people, but the number of applications also grew. Sorting through hundreds of profiles became another big challenge. Many teams found themselves spending more time reviewing resumes than actually speaking to candidates.

Now, companies are turning to Voice AI for Hiring to help them manage this workload. Instead of depending only on human effort, they use AI agents that can call, speak, and understand candidates automatically. These voice agents can ask questions, collect answers, and even schedule follow-ups, all without manual effort.

## **The Problem with Traditional Hiring**

Even with all the digital tools available today, many hiring teams still rely on old methods. Recruiters spend long hours calling candidates, confirming details, and setting up interviews. Most of these tasks are repetitive and leave very little time for real evaluation.

When companies receive hundreds of applications, screening every candidate manually becomes nearly impossible. It leads to delays, missed calls, and slow decision-making. Sometimes, the best candidates lose interest or accept other offers before the process is complete.

Traditional hiring also depends heavily on human judgment, which can lead to bias or inconsistency. Two recruiters may rate the same candidate differently simply because they notice different things in a conversation.

## **Understanding Voice AI for Hiring**
