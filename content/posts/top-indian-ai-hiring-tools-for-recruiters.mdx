### **Talview**

Talview is an Indian AI-based platform mainly used for online assessments and video interviews. It helps recruiters test technical and communication skills through recorded responses and secure online exams. The tool uses **AI proctoring** to prevent cheating and ensure fairness, which makes it useful for remote hiring and campus recruitment.

For Indian recruiters, Talview works best when large groups of candidates need to be tested quickly without arranging physical exams. It reduces manual effort and keeps assessments consistent.

However, Talview focuses mainly on testing and evaluation. It does not manage full recruitment workflows or engage candidates through natural conversations. While it brings value to the assessment stage, its role is limited compared to more complete voice-AI hiring platforms. Talview is a good choice for structured skill testing, but recruiters looking for an all-round AI hiring partner often prefer more conversational tools.

### **Zoho Recruit**

Zoho Recruit is an Indian AI recruitment software that helps businesses manage their hiring process more efficiently. It offers features like job posting, candidate tracking, and automated email communication, all from a single dashboard. Recruiters can use it to collect resumes, schedule interviews, and monitor progress at every stage of hiring.

One of its biggest strengths is its integration with other Zoho products such as Zoho HR and Zoho CRM, which creates a smooth flow of information across teams. This makes it a practical choice for small and mid-sized companies that want an organised and affordable recruitment solution.

Zoho Recruit focuses on automation and centralisation rather than advanced assessments or voice-based interactions. It is well-suited for recruiters who want to reduce manual work, keep data organised, and maintain a clear overview of all open positions in one simple and easy-to-use system.

### **HireVue**

HireVue is a global AI interview tool that helps companies conduct and evaluate video interviews at scale. It uses artificial intelligence to analyse candidates’ speech, tone, and facial expressions during recorded interviews. Recruiters can review these insights to understand how candidates communicate and respond under real interview conditions.

The platform is especially useful for large organisations and multinational companies that need to manage thousands of interviews efficiently. With video-based assessments and structured question formats, HireVue ensures that every candidate gets the same evaluation experience.

For Indian recruiters, HireVue can be a good option for remote hiring or when teams are spread across different locations. It saves time on scheduling and allows interview data to be stored and shared easily. However, its strength lies mainly in analysis and structure, making it better suited for formal interview processes than for day-to-day screening or early engagement.

### **Pymetrics**

Pymetrics is an AI-powered assessment platform that helps recruiters understand the personality, behaviour, and potential of candidates. Instead of traditional tests, it uses short and engaging games to measure traits such as memory, problem-solving, attention, and emotional intelligence. These insights help recruiters identify candidates who are likely to succeed in a particular role or company culture.

For Indian recruiters, Pymetrics can be helpful when hiring for roles that require strong soft skills or adaptability. It provides a fairer way to evaluate candidates beyond resumes and academic scores, which is valuable in today’s diverse job market.

The platform focuses more on understanding human behaviour than on managing the overall hiring process. It is often used along with other tools for screening or interviews. Pymetrics works well for companies that want to build balanced teams and make hiring decisions based on data-driven insights rather than intuition alone.

### **hireEZ**

hireEZ, earlier known as Hiretual, is an AI recruiting platform that focuses on candidate sourcing and outreach. It helps recruiters find qualified candidates from multiple platforms such as LinkedIn, GitHub, and job boards, collecting data into one easy-to-use interface. The platform’s AI search function can filter profiles based on experience, skills, and availability, saving recruiters many hours of manual searching.

For Indian recruiters, hireEZ can be useful in industries where specialised or technical talent is in high demand. It allows hiring teams to build targeted lists and connect with potential candidates through automated email campaigns. The tool also provides insights about market trends and candidate behaviour, helping recruiters plan their hiring strategy better.

hireEZ works best for sourcing and lead generation rather than full recruitment automation. It is a practical choice for recruiters who want to widen their reach and build stronger candidate pipelines quickly.

### **Fetcher**

Fetcher is an AI recruitment platform that helps recruiters find and engage with potential candidates more efficiently. It combines artificial intelligence with human review to source profiles that match specific job requirements. The tool automates much of the early hiring work by searching across online platforms, screening candidates, and even sending personalised emails for outreach.

For Indian recruiters, Fetcher can be useful when hiring for multiple roles at once or when building a talent pipeline. It saves time by handling repetitive tasks such as searching and shortlisting while keeping candidate communication active. Recruiters receive a steady flow of pre-screened candidates, which helps speed up the hiring process.

While Fetcher focuses strongly on sourcing and outreach, it does not cover deeper assessments or interviews. It is best suited for teams that already have a clear recruitment process in place and need help finding the right people faster.
