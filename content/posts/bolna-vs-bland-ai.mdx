## **Bolna vs Bland Feature Comparison**

Both platforms aim to make voice automation easier for businesses, but they differ in several key areas. From pricing to setup time, each platform offers a different experience in how agents are created, managed, and supported. Understanding these differences can help businesses choose a solution that matches their goals and resources. Let us take a closer look.

### **Pricing and Accessibility**

Pricing is one of the clearest differences between the two platforms. Bolna starts at **$0.06 per minute** on pay-as-you-go, with speech-to-text, the LLM and text-to-speech included when your agent uses the bundled models. Telephony and the platform fee are added per minute. Bland's Start plan costs $0.14 per minute with no platform fee, and its Build plan costs $0.12 per minute plus $299 a month. Bland's rate includes the LLM, speech-to-text and text-to-speech, and telephony is billed separately.

Phone numbers can be purchased directly through **Bolna for $5 per month**. Bland's Start plan includes an inbound number, which Bland values at $15 a month. The lower starting rate makes Bolna a practical choice for businesses that handle a large number of calls each day.

Bolna also provides flexibility through [on-premise deployment](https://www.bolna.ai/blog/enterprise-voice-ai-on-prem) options, allowing enterprises to manage their data securely within their own systems. This feature is particularly valuable for companies with strict compliance or privacy needs. Bland also offers on-prem or VPC deployment, along with a BAA, SSO and data residency, on its Enterprise plan.

### **Integrations and Customization**

Integrations decide how well a platform fits into a company’s existing tools. Bolna offers direct platform access with easy setup for multiple integrations including Twilio, Plivo, Make.com, and Cal.com. Users can also build custom workflows without technical knowledge.

Bolna supports 40+ languages, including Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil and Telugu, which makes it a good fit for audiences across India. Bland works with your existing Twilio account or a SIP trunk from any provider, and it supports porting your numbers.

### **Agent Functions and Performance**

Bolna offers a strong set of agent features such as appointment booking, availability checking, call transfer, hang-up options, and even the ability to create custom functions. Agents can provide detailed call summaries, transcripts, and recordings directly on the platform.

Bolna targets under 600 milliseconds end to end, so conversations feel natural and quick. Bland agents follow the call logic you build in Conversational Pathways.

### Support and Setup

Bolna offers help through its Slack community and support email, and the Pilot plan adds a WhatsApp channel with the team and four 1:1 calls. The platform is designed to be easy to set up, allowing teams to deploy agents within minutes.

Bland's Enterprise plan includes a Slack channel with the Bland team and Forward Deployed Engineers, who build a customer's first agent. Its Start and Build plans list account and billing support.
