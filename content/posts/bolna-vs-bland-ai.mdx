Both Bolna and Bland AI show how far voice technology has come in changing customer communication. #### **Overview of Bland AI**

Bland AI is a voice automation platform designed for basic call handling and simple voice tasks. It focuses on creating agents that can manage routine conversations such as confirming bookings or answering short questions. The platform is mainly built for English-speaking users and is often used for small projects or testing voice automation ideas.

Bland AI works well for teams that want to explore voice automation on a smaller level or for those testing early-stage use cases.

## **Bolna vs Bland Feature Comparison**

Both platforms aim to make voice automation easier for businesses, but they differ in several key areas. From pricing to setup time, each platform offers a different experience in how agents are created, managed, and supported. Understanding these differences can help businesses choose a solution that matches their goals and resources. Let us take a closer look.

Pricing is one of the clearest differences between the two platforms. Bolna offers a cost-effective model, starting at about **$0.03 per minute of calls**, while Bland AI costs around $0.09 per minute.

Phone numbers can be purchased directly through **Bolna for as low as $2 per month**, compared to Bland AI’s $15 per month. The lower price makes Bolna more suitable for businesses that handle a large number of calls each day.

### **Integrations and Customization**

Integrations decide how well a platform fits into a company’s existing tools. Bolna offers direct platform access with easy setup for multiple integrations including Twilio, Plivo, Make.com, and Cal.com. Users can also build custom workflows without technical knowledge.

Bolna supports multilingual and regional languages, which makes it more adaptable to diverse audiences. This includes Indian languages such as Hindi, Tamil, and Bengali, along with English and other global languages. Bland AI, on the other hand, offers limited integration options and works mainly with Twilio and Zapier. Its setup requires more technical steps, which can slow down deployment.

### **Agent Functions and Performance**

Bolna offers a strong set of agent features such as appointment booking, availability checking, call transfer, hang-up options, and even the ability to create custom functions. Agents can provide detailed call summaries, transcripts, and recordings directly on the platform.

Latency is low, usually between 500 to 800 milliseconds, allowing conversations to feel natural and quick. Bland AI supports core functions like call handling and appointment booking but has fewer options for customization and slightly higher latency.

### Support and Setup

Bolna provides 24-hour customer support with onboarding calls, live demos, and chat assistance. The platform is designed to be easy to set up, allowing teams to deploy agents within minutes.

Bland AI offers basic support through email and community channels but lacks live guidance during setup. The difference in support quality and response time can make a major impact on how quickly teams can start using the platform effectively.
