A few years ago, artificial intelligence felt distant from everyday work. Today, it’s becoming part of almost every team. From handling customer support to reviewing job applications, AI systems now take on tasks that once needed human attention.

These systems are known as AI agents. They are programs that can understand information, make decisions, and complete tasks on their own. Some of them can even talk and listen like people do. These are called Voice AI agents, and they are changing the way companies communicate and operate every day.

NVIDIA’s CEO Jensen Huang once said, “[The IT department of every company will be the HR department of AI agents.](https://www.youtube.com/live/K4qQtPpSn-k)” His words describe a future that’s already taking shape. Businesses are beginning to treat AI agents as digital coworkers, tools that don’t just process data but actively help teams work better.

## **How IT Work Is Changing with AI**

The work of IT teams is changing fast. For years, their main focus was maintaining systems, fixing software problems, and managing networks. Now, with artificial intelligence becoming part of every system, IT teams are doing something new, they are guiding how these intelligent systems actually work.

One of the biggest changes is the rise of AI agents. These are programs that can act on their own, make decisions, and carry out tasks without waiting for human input. In many companies, AI agents handle things like system monitoring, ticket responses, or data checks automatically. Instead of just tools, they’re becoming digital team members that help IT teams manage larger workloads.

![](/images/posts/voice-ai-changing-it-and-hr/ai-background-business-technology-digital-transformation-1024x683.jpg)

Among these agents, Voice AI has become one of the most useful and visible kinds. Voice AI agents can listen, understand, and talk to people naturally. This makes them perfect for tasks that involve real-time communication, such as answering questions, guiding employees, or assisting customers.

In India, Indian Voice AI systems are becoming especially valuable. They can understand local languages and accents, which helps IT and support teams connect with users in a more personal way. Whether it’s helping employees reset a password or assisting a customer through a service call, these agents are becoming part of how IT works every day.

By managing both machines and intelligent agents, IT teams are stepping into a new kind of role, one that’s less about fixing problems and more about managing intelligent systems that work alongside people.

## **How IT and HR Are Working Together with AI**

Artificial intelligence is bringing IT and HR closer than ever. In the past, these two teams worked in different ways. IT managed systems and tools. HR managed people and culture. Now they often work together to make sure both humans and AI systems can do their best work.

IT teams understand how AI works and how to keep it running safely. HR teams understand how people think, what keeps them engaged, and how to create a positive work experience. When both sides work together, companies can use AI in ways that truly help people instead of replacing them.

In hiring, this teamwork is already visible. IT teams help set up and manage [AI hiring tools](https://www.bolna.ai/blog/recruiting-tools-in-2025) that can screen resumes or hold short voice interviews. HR teams decide what skills and qualities to look for and how to make the process fair. IT makes sure the systems work correctly, while HR ensures that the experience feels human.

This kind of teamwork also helps prevent mistakes. IT can monitor how AI handles data, and HR can review results to make sure decisions stay fair and unbiased. Working together keeps technology transparent and trustworthy.

Voice AI makes this partnership even stronger. When an AI system speaks to candidates or employees, it represents the company’s tone and values. HR decides how that voice should sound, and IT builds the system to make it possible.

Voice AI is not just another tool. It helps teams work faster, communicate better, and make decisions with more confidence. For IT and HR teams, it opens new ways to save time and improve the overall work experience.

-   **Saves time on repetitive work**: Voice AI agents can handle routine tasks like answering questions, collecting information, or scheduling calls. This allows IT and HR teams to focus on more important work instead of spending hours on simple follow-ups.
-   **Keeps communication consistent**: Whether it is talking to job candidates or helping employees, Voice AI ensures every message stays clear and on-brand. This helps HR maintain the right tone and IT maintain reliability across systems.

## **Preparing for the Future of AI at Work**

Artificial intelligence is now part of daily work, and its presence will only grow in the years to come. For most companies, the goal is not to replace people but to help them work better with technology. The future of work will depend on how well humans and AI can learn to understand and support each other.

The first step is to build awareness. Everyone in an organisation should understand what AI can do and where human judgment is still needed. When people know how a system works, they are more likely to trust it and use it effectively.

Clear rules are also important. Every AI system should operate with transparency and fairness. IT and HR teams can work together to make sure data is used responsibly, decisions are reviewed, and technology remains a tool that supports people.

Culture will play a big role in this change. AI can only help when teams are open to learning and comfortable with new ways of working. Encouraging collaboration and communication helps people see AI as a partner, not a threat.

The final step is to choose tools that are reliable and easy to use. Starting small often leads to better long-term results. Platforms like Bolna, an Indian Voice AI solution, make it possible for teams to begin using AI without needing large budgets or technical complexity.

Preparing for the future of AI at work is not just about technology. It is about building workplaces where people and systems work side by side, combining human understanding with the speed and intelligence of AI.
