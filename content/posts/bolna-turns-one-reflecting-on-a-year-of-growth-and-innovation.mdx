What a journey it’s been! Bolna recently celebrated its first anniversary, and what an incredible, action-packed year it has been. From landing (and losing) contracts to making strategic pivots, building a self-serve dashboard that occasionally buckled under unexpected traffic, and raising funds to assemble a stellar team, Bolna’s ride has been nothing short of exhilarating.

The chaos of 2024 ended on a high note. In just the last six months, Bolna served over **1,300 users across 12+ countries**. Our platform powered conversations ranging from sales and customer support to helping restaurants in Malaysia hire staff efficiently and confirming attendees for a church event in Michigan.

![Bolna celebrating one year of growth and product milestones](/images/posts/bolna-turns-one-reflecting-on-a-year-of-growth-and-innovation/iuhgiu.jpg)

## Lessons Learned in 2024

For Bolna, 2024 was a year of learning. Our biggest challenge? Delivering customer delight while implementing complex agentic systems.

While some of our tech-savvy customers successfully built seamless workflows using Bolna’s **Voice AI Infrastructure**, others struggled to achieve their goals. It quickly became apparent that simply offering a platform and stepping back wasn’t enough. To help our customers truly succeed, we needed to do more.

## Enter 2025: “The Agents of Bolna”

This year marks the launch of something transformative: **The Agents of Bolna**. These are no ordinary AI prompts. These fine-tuned agents are designed to execute end-to-end workflows with the efficiency of ten humans—all at a fraction of the cost.

Here’s what these agents bring to the table:

-   **Personalized Interactions:** Tailoring prompts for every user.
-   **Multi-Modal Conversations:** Driving meaningful, efficient interactions.
-   **Actionable Insights:** Analyzing each interaction for data-driven decisions.
-   **Seamless Conversations:** With ultra-low latency (~300ms) and advanced interruption handling.

Our mission for 2025 isn’t just to simplify AI. It’s to empower you to leverage AI and make your life simpler.

## A Year of Winning Ahead

If 2024 was about learning, 2025 is the year of winning. We’re gearing up to soft-launch our agents soon, and we couldn’t be more excited about the possibilities.

To our users, supporters, and stellar team: Thank you for being part of this journey. And a special shoutout to ChatGPT for being our steadfast companion during long, challenging nights!

Stay tuned as we take Bolna to new heights. Here’s to 2025 and beyond!
